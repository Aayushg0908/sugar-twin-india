Personalized Physiology Twin

Each patient's carb sensitivity, recovery time and walking effect are learned from week 1 (logged meals + CGM only) and tested on unseen week-2 meals. 5,000 of 5,000 patients had enough clean logged meals to fit; walking effect learned from own data for 2,020, population value otherwise.

Week 2: predicting the glucose rise after a new meal (9,316 meals of test patients)

Each patient's own estimate is blended with their cohort's typical value; the blend strength (k = 8 pseudo-meals) was chosen on training patients only.
