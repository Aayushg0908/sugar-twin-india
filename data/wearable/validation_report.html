Wearable & CGM Stream — Validation Report

5,000 patients × 14 days × 288 readings/day. CGM (glucose), smartwatch (heart rate, steps, sleep stage), and an event log (meals, medication doses, exercise). Fully synthetic and driven by each patient's EHR twin parameters.

1. Agreement with the EHR

CGM-derived GMI vs latest lab HbA1c in T2D: correlation 0.93, mean difference -0.26%, 35% of patients differ by ≥ 0.5% (real-world studies report about 28%).

3. Comparison with real patient CGM data

ShanghaiT2DM (Zhao et al., Scientific Data 2023, CC BY 4.0): 109 recordings from T2D patients, 15-min CGM. Simulated T2D patients were matched to each real recording on mean glucose (10 nearest each). The Shanghai cohort is mostly hospital patients (about half on insulin) eating Chinese meals at earlier times, so meal timing and the shape of the daily curve are expected to differ.
