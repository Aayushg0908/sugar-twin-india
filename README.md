# Sugar Twin India: a Digital Twin that Predicts Glucose Spikes Before They Happen

**Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026** · Team **guptaaayush0908** · Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology
Aayush Gupta (team leader) · Dhruv Gandhi · Divyansh Singh

A virtual replica of each Type 2 diabetes patient that fuses electronic health records with CGM and smartwatch data,
predicts glucose spikes and lows up to 2 hours ahead, and lets a doctor test a meal, a walk or a missed dose on the patient's own physiology.

All project files are in **[guptaaayush0908_Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology/](guptaaayush0908_Maharaja%20Agrasen%20Institute%20of%20Technology/)**, following the submission naming rule (Team Name_College Name).
